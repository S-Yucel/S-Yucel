### 🏠 Ommini sector editions

Instead of a blank prompt box, each edition starts from the data a professional already has and produces everything they need to publish.

**Ommini Emlak** — for real-estate agents (live):
- Structured listing form modelled on Türkiye's largest listing portal, with autosave
- Public listing page per property — photo gallery, map, WhatsApp contact and link previews, in TR / EN / AR (RTL)
- AI listing copy for portals and social media in three languages, with a real-estate glossary
- Listing video from the agent's photos — vertical and horizontal, AI narration or the agent's own voice, background music
- Authorization-expiry reminders and monthly listing quotas per plan

More sector editions are on the way.
